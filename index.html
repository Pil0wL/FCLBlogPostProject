What we do

Solar installation

Rooftop and ground-mount systems for residential, commercial and industrial properties.

Design and consultation

Load studies, layout, and financial modeling, including net-metering applications.

Battery storage

Lithium-ion systems that keep power on during outages and cut peak-hour costs.

Electrical engineering

Switchgear, grid-tie, and facility wiring by licensed professional electrical engineers.

Audits and live dashboards that show where energy is used and wasted.

About VoltEdge

Tagline: Powering Progress. Engineering a Sustainable Future.

Founded in 2016 in Dasmariñas, Cavite, VoltEdge is a renewable energy engineering firm registered with the SEC and accredited with the DOE. About 210 employees serve Luzon, Visayas and Mindanao.

Mission

Deliver reliable, efficient and affordable clean energy that Filipino households and businesses can trust.

Vision

A Philippines where renewable power is the default choice for every building.

Values

Safety first, engineering integrity, customer partnership, environmental stewardship.

Departments

Engineering and Design, Installation, Energy Services, Sales, Finance, People and Safety.
